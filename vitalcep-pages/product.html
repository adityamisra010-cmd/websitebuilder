About This Product

Vitalcep Cordyceps Militaris is grown using a controlled indoor cultivation process that ensures 100% traceability from spore to shelf. Every batch is tested for purity — no heavy metals, no pesticides, no adulterants.

Our ultra-fine milling process ensures maximum surface area — which means maximum absorption into your bloodstream, and faster results.
