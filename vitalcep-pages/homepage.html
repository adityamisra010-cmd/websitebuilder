The Active Compounds

NOT MAGIC.
BIOCHEMISTRY.

Cordyceps Militaris works because of two extraordinary compounds: Cordycepin and Adenosine. These aren't folklore — they're molecules studied in peer-reviewed research worldwide.

C

Cordycepin (3'-Deoxyadenosine)

Structurally similar to adenosine, cordycepin mimics cellular signalling to increase ATP energy production and inhibit inflammatory pathways. Directly supports endurance and immunity.

A

Adenosine

A key regulator of cellular energy transfer. Supports oxygen delivery to muscles, reduces fatigue signals, and enhances the body's ability to sustain high-intensity output for longer.

β

Beta-Glucans

Polysaccharide compounds that directly stimulate Natural Killer (NK) cells and macrophages — the frontline soldiers of your immune system. Measurable immunity boost.

How long until I notice results?

Most customers notice subtle energy and clarity improvements within 7–10 days. Full endurance and immunity benefits become prominent at 3–4 weeks with consistent daily use. For best results, treat it like training — consistency beats intensity.

What's the difference between Militaris and Sinensis?

Cordyceps Sinensis grows parasitically on caterpillars in the wild — extremely rare, expensive, and near-impossible to source ethically. Cordyceps Militaris (what we use) is cultivated in a controlled environment, has higher cordycepin content, is 100% vegan, and is backed by more published research. It's scientifically superior for supplementation.

Can I take it with other supplements?

Yes. Vitalcep Cordyceps stacks well with protein powders, creatine, ashwagandha, and most adaptogens. Many customers mix it with their pre-workout or morning coffee. If you're on prescription medication, consult your physician before adding any supplement.

What's the recommended dosage?

Start with 1g per day (approximately ½ teaspoon of powder) for the first two weeks. After your body adapts, you can increase to 2g for enhanced performance effects. Best taken 30 minutes before training or with your first meal of the day.
