What is the difference between Militaris and Sinensis?

Cordyceps Sinensis grows parasitically on caterpillars in the wild — extremely rare, near-impossible to source ethically. Cordyceps Militaris (what we use) is cultivated in controlled environments, has significantly higher cordycepin content, is 100% vegan, and is backed by substantially more published research. It is scientifically superior for supplementation.

What is the recommended dosage?
