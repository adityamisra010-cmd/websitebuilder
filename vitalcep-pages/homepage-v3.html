The Active Compounds

Not Magic.
Biochemistry.

Cordyceps Militaris works because of three specific compounds. These are not folklore. They are molecules studied in peer-reviewed research worldwide.

C

Cordycepin (3'-Deoxyadenosine)

Mimics cellular signalling to increase ATP energy production and inhibit inflammatory pathways. Directly supports endurance and immunity.

A

Adenosine

A key regulator of cellular energy transfer. Supports oxygen delivery to muscles, reduces fatigue signals, and helps the body sustain high-intensity output.

β

Beta-Glucans

Polysaccharides that stimulate Natural Killer (NK) cells and macrophages, the frontline of your immune system. Measurable immunity boost with consistent use.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

When do I start seeing results?

Most customers notice a shift in energy by the end of Week 1. Stamina improvements show up around Week 2 to 3. The full adaptogenic effect builds over 5 to 6 weeks of consistent daily use. Cordyceps is not a stimulant. Treat it like training: consistency matters more than timing.

Militaris vs. Sinensis: what's the difference?

Cordyceps Sinensis grows parasitically on caterpillars in the wild. It is extremely rare, expensive and near-impossible to source ethically. Cordyceps Militaris is cultivated in a controlled environment, has higher cordycepin content, is 100% vegan, and is backed by more published research. It is the scientifically superior choice for supplementation.

Can I take it with other supplements?

Yes. Vitalcep Cordyceps stacks well with protein powders, creatine, ashwagandha, and most adaptogens. Many customers mix the powder into their pre-workout or morning coffee. If you are on prescription medication, check with your physician before adding any supplement.

What is the recommended dosage?

1g per day for the first two weeks. After your body adapts, you can move to 2g for stronger performance effects. Best taken 30 minutes before training or with your first meal. The powder dissolves easily in water, smoothies or warm drinks.
