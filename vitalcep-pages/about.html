How We Started

THE ORIGIN

It started with a question: why do elite athletes in China and Nepal consistently outperform everyone at altitude?

The answer kept coming back to one thing — Cordyceps Militaris. A mushroom that grows in the Himalayas, known for centuries for its ability to enhance oxygen utilisation, energy, and physical resilience.

We spent two years studying the science, visiting cultivation labs, and testing different strains before we were satisfied with what we had. We weren't going to release anything we wouldn't take ourselves daily.

Vitalcep launched with a simple promise: pure Cordyceps Militaris, cultivated with integrity, backed by science, delivered to serious people at an honest price.

We're still that team. We still make the same promise.

WHY VITALCEP IS DIFFERENT

Fruiting Body Only

Many brands sell mycelium on grain — cheap to produce, low in actual Cordyceps compounds. We use only the fruiting body: the part that actually contains cordycepin, adenosine, and beta-glucans.

Every Batch Tested

Third-party lab testing for heavy metals, pesticides, microbial contamination, and active compound verification. Certificates available on request. We don't ship anything we haven't tested.

Controlled Environment Grown

Our Cordyceps is grown indoors in a controlled environment — same temperature, humidity, and substrate every cycle. Consistent quality, no seasonal variation, no contamination risk from wild harvesting.

Honest Pricing

We don't inflate our price to make it seem more premium. We charge a fair price for a genuinely excellent product. We'd rather have you reorder than overcharge you once.
